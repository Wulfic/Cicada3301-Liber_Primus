### Key Technical Notes for Statistical Modeling

1. **29-character alphabet:** Gematria Primus maps Anglo-Saxon runes to 29 symbols (A–Y + AE, OE, IO, EA). No J, Q, X, or Z.
2. **F-skip rule:** When plaintext letter is F, the cipher outputs literal ᚠ and the key counter does NOT advance. Critical for Vigenère/shift ciphers.
3. **U/V merger:** Both U and V map to the same rune ᚢ — hence "EUERY" for "EVERY."
4. **No word boundaries in cipher:** Dots (•) separate words in rune text but these are stripped during encryption analysis.
5. **LP2 mirrors LP1:** Pages 58–68 substantially repeat the content of pages 00–13, using different (generally simpler) ciphers.
6. **Unsolved pages 20–54:** This is the bulk of unsolved material (~35 pages). High IoC values suggest substitution-type ciphers, but no key has been found.
